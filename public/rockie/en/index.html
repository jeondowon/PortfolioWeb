WHAT IS ROCKIE?

An easy little
desktop companion

Rockie is a small pet rock that spends the day inside your computer.
Watch a stone that mirrors you grow up.

It follows your cursor

It strolls along the bottom of your screen, trailing after your mouse.
You can also tell it to stay put.

It reacts when you pet it

Click to strike up a conversation.
It warns you when the battery runs low, and sometimes speaks first.

It does more than sit there

Keyboard cleaning mode, focus mode, nap mode, and more.
A system monitor shows how your computer is doing.

It grows as you answer

Answer the personality question it asks each day, and your pebble
evolves into one of four stones that mirror you.

FIND YOUR STONE

Four stones, one of them you

Answer the daily personality questions and your pebble settles into one of the four.
From there it passes through a metamorphic form and becomes a gem.
