The demo uses seconds 49–53, after most of the crowd has crossed.
The 1920x1080, 30fps source is cropped to 720x404 at (1050, 580),
without upscaling. Motion-compensated interpolation produces a 60fps
delivery clip (240 frames over 4 seconds); this is not native 60fps footage.
Encoded with H.264 CRF 17, yuv420p, faststart, and no audio.

Reproduction from the original downloaded MP4:
ffmpeg -ss 48.9 -i source.mp4 -t 4.2 -vf "crop=720:404:1050:580,minterpolate=fps=60:mi_mode=mci:mc_mode=aobmc:me_mode=bidir:vsbmc=1,trim=start=0.1:duration=4,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS" -an -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 17 -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -map_metadata -1 crosswalk.mp4
